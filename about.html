Our story

A restaurant with roots, a point of view.

Jade Wok is a contemporary Chinese restaurant shaped by family recipes, modern craft and the pleasure of gathering well.

Home/About
A living tradition

We cook to keep the conversation going

The best traditions are not preserved in glass. They are practised, adapted and shared.

Jade Wok began as a family kitchen project in 2008, when Lin Wei started cooking the food he remembered alongside the flavours he was still discovering. Today, the restaurant carries that same spirit into a room designed for modern city life: open-hearted, ingredient-led and unafraid of a little heat.

Our menu moves between delicate dim sum and confident wok cooking, between the precision of a tea ceremony and the joyful mess of a shared table. There is no performance for its own sake. Only good ingredients, honest technique and the feeling that someone thought about your evening.

What guides us

Three ways we keep the flame alive

01

Respect the ingredient

We build menus around seasonality, texture and provenance, letting every ingredient speak in its clearest voice.

02

Make it by hand

From dumpling folds to fermented sauces, the small human gestures are what give a dish its character.

03

Leave room for joy

Precision matters, but hospitality is the point. We want the last bite to feel as good as the first.

From market to table

Good cooking starts long before the wok.

Each morning begins with a walk through the market: a search for the right greens, the sweetest shellfish, the aromatics that will carry a broth. Our suppliers are small, our standards are high and our waste is designed down wherever we can.

The timeline

A few chapters so far

2008

A family kitchen project

Lin Wei begins hosting Sunday suppers built around handmade dumplings and slow-cooked broths.

2013

The first Jade Wok opens

A small Downtown dining room becomes a home for the food, people and stories that inspired it.

2018

The tasting menu arrives

A seasonal sequence gives the kitchen space to explore regional techniques and new ingredients.

2022

Private dining takes shape

The Jade Room opens for celebrations, chef’s tables and long evenings with friends.

2026

A new chapter

Today, the work continues: more curious, more generous and always connected to the table.
