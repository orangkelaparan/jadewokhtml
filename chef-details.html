The philosophy

Keep the fire honest

“I am interested in the moment when a familiar flavour becomes your own.”

Lin Wei grew up in a home where the kitchen was always the centre of the day. After years moving through Cantonese roast kitchens, Sichuan restaurants and modern tasting menus, he returned to the simple question that still guides his work: how can a dish honour where it came from and still belong to the people eating it now?

At Jade Wok, Lin builds menus around clarity. A broth should taste like itself. A dumpling should hold its balance. A plate should invite you in before it asks you to think.
