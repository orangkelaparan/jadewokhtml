The essentials

Everything you need before you arrive

Can’t find what you are looking for? Send us a note →

01Do I need a reservation?+

Reservations are recommended, particularly for dinner and weekends. We always keep a few places for walk-ins when we can.

02Do you welcome walk-ins?+

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome subject to availability. The bar and chef counter are often the best options for a spontaneous visit.

03Can you accommodate dietary requirements?+

We can accommodate many dietary requirements with advance notice. Please include details in your reservation request and speak with your server.

04Do you have vegetarian dishes?+

Yes. Our menu includes dedicated vegetarian dishes, and the kitchen can suggest seasonal adjustments.

05How does private dining work?+

Choose a room, share a few details about your occasion and our events team will respond with availability and menu options.

06Are children welcome?+

Children are welcome during all services. We can provide high chairs and help guide families toward shareable dishes.

07What is the dress code?+

Smart casual is perfect. We want you to feel considered, not constrained.

08Is parking available?+

Street parking and nearby public garages are available around both fictional locations. Please check local signage when visiting.

09Can you host large parties?+

Our private dining rooms host groups of 6–24. For larger events, please contact the events team.

10How should I tell you about an allergy?+

Mention it in your booking request and repeat it when you arrive. We take allergies seriously and will explain any limitations.

11Do you sell gift cards?+

Gift cards are a planned customization area for the template. Connect the CTA to your preferred payment or commerce provider.

12What is the cancellation policy?+

Please give us at least 24 hours’ notice when possible. Your final policy should be added here by the restaurant owner.
