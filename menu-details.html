Signature
Peking Duck

$46 for two

A Jade Wok ritual: lacquered crisp skin, tender meat, warm bao, cucumber and the plum sauce we make in-house.

The details

Built in layers
of patience.

We air-dry the duck overnight, brush it with a light maltose glaze, then roast it until the skin is brittle and the meat stays lush. At the table, it arrives with hand-steamed bao and the bright crunch of cucumber.

IngredientsDuck, maltose, five spice, cucumber, scallion, wheat flour, plum
DietaryContains gluten. Please ask about allergen adaptations.
ServingDesigned for two guests; allow 25 minutes from order.
